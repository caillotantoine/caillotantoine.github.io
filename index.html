Antoine CAILLOT

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Kyoto University’s Academic Center for Computing and Media Studies (ACCMS), in the Image Informatics Laboratory led by Prof. Takuya Funatomi. My research interests include computer vision, computational imaging, image processing, visual SLAM, omnidirectional vision, emergent imaging sensors, and cooperative perception. Previously, I worked on robust perception and bio-inspired robot navigation at the CNRS-AIST Joint Robotics Laboratory. Outside research, I enjoy (astro)photography and computer science side projects.
