Dual-Exposure for Improving Omnidirectional Visual Simultaneous Localization And Mapping

Antoine Caillot, Eva Goichon, Ryusuke Sagawa, and Guillaume Caron.

This work investigates dual-exposure image acquisition for robust omnidirectional visual SLAM in challenging lighting conditions. The system combines short and long exposures to preserve visual information across large illumination changes, including indoor-to-outdoor transitions.
